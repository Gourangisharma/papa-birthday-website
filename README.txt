HAPPY BIRTHDAY PAPA — WEBSITE
================================

HOW TO VIEW IT
Just double-click "index.html" — it opens in any browser, no internet
needed except for the two Google Fonts used (Fraunces + Caveat + Outfit).

ADDING YOUR REAL PHOTOS (2 minutes)
------------------------------------
Right now the "images" folder has placeholder cards labeled with where
each real photo goes. Save your WhatsApp photos into the "images" folder
using these exact names (this replaces the placeholder automatically):

  img1.jpg   -> Papa & Mummy at Fatehpur Sikri (crowd/courtyard photo)
  img2.jpg   -> Papa in the yellow marigold turban
  img3.jpg   -> Papa & daughter selfie at Fatehpur Sikri
  img4.jpg   -> Black & white solo portrait of Papa
  img5.jpg   -> Papa & Mummy at the misty white temple
  img6.jpg   -> Papa & Mummy in the golf cart/buggy
  img7.jpg   -> Papa & Mummy near Panch Mahal (teal kurti, white shirt)
  img8.jpg   -> Close selfie under the chandelier (dinner photo)
  img9.jpg   -> Papa & Mummy on the green lawn, golden hour
  img10.jpg  -> Papa with two kids years ago (throwback photo)

Tip: any image editor / Preview / Photos app lets you "Save As" or
"Rename" — just make sure the file ends in .jpg and matches a name above.
JPEG or PNG both work, just keep the same filename+extension pattern
(if you use .png, update the "src" in index.html for that image to .png).

WHAT'S INSIDE
--------------
index.html   - the 6 chapters (Hero, Memory Lane, People Who Love You,
               Little Things We Love, Wishes & Blessings, Make a Wish)
style.css    - all the styling (dark night-sky theme, marigold/rose glow)
script.js    - starfield, confetti, scroll reveals, blow-out-candles game
images/      - your photos live here

CUSTOMIZING THE WORDS
-----------------------
Open index.html in any text editor — every message (Mummy's note, your
note, the trait cards) is plain text inside the file, easy to find and
edit. Search for "Mummy" or "Your Daughter" to jump to those spots.
